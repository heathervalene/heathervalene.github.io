Hi, I'm Heather 👋

I'm a software developer based in New York City. I'd call myself a jack-of-all-trades, master of none — I've built full-stack projects but lean toward the front end.

I spent 12 years in tech before I wrote a line of code — six of them at startups from Series A through unicorn status, the last five in leadership roles on the business side, building and leading client-facing teams around retention. After years working closely with end users, product, and engineering, I wanted to get closer to how the software actually got built. I took a 400-hour software engineering bootcamp to fast-track my skills, and now I build the kind of software I used to sell.

I like learning new languages and frameworks on both ends of the stack, but I keep coming back to the front end — especially React, paired with something like Bootstrap to keep the build and the design moving together toward a polished result.

Outside of work, I'm usually out exploring what New York City has to offer — restaurants, live shows, neighborhood spots worth a second visit. Summers, my husband and I head upstate to hike, and together we run a small dog-sitting business on the side.
